About StanceBoy Motors

Our Story

Founded in 2020 in Pretoria, StanceBoy Motors started with one goal: to build the best stance and performance cars in Gauteng. What began in a small garage has grown into a trusted shop for car enthusiasts who want quality, style, and power.

Our Mission

To deliver top-tier vehicle customization and servicing while building a community of passionate car lovers. We don’t just build cars, we build StanceBoy culture.

Our Vision

To be the #1 go-to customization shop in South Africa, known for innovation, craftsmanship, and customer experience.

Meet The Team

Our team Members of certified mechanics and customizers are obsessed with cars. From coilovers to engine tunes, we treat every car like it’s our own.
